Part one

The website

A custom built site for Advantage Tennis. Not a template, not a page builder subscription. Designed and written around how people actually find and book a coach.

Price

What is included

  • Full custom design and build, written copy included
  • Built mobile first, because that is where almost all of your enquiries will come from
  • Your sessions, your coaching, your pricing, laid out so a parent understands them in seconds
  • Enquiry and booking routed wherever you want it to land
  • Basic on page search setup so you can be found for your own name and your area
  • Google Analytics connected so you can see what is actually happening
  • A proper handover, with everything in accounts that belong to you

What is not included at this price

  • Online payment processing or a full booking engine with card payments. If you want that, it is a separate piece of work and I will price it for you properly rather than absorbing it.
  • Ongoing marketing. That is part two below, and it is entirely optional.
  • Photography. I will use what you send me.

Changes after launch

You said on the call you expect to want a change roughly every six months. That is exactly how this should work for a business your size.

Small changes, a new price, a new session time, swapping a photo, are quoted individually and will be small. You will always have the number before I start. There is no retainer required and no minimum.
