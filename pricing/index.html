The 12-Month Arc

What the retainer actually looks like over a year.

Not a subscription fee, an appreciating asset. Here's the illustrative shape of how it compounds, not a guarantee, a realistic pattern.

Months 1 to 3 · Getting Found +

The basics get fixed so Google can properly see you, the first ads go live, and your first pages start showing up when people nearby search for what you do.

  • SEO: anything stopping Google listing you fixed, the site made fast on phones, your local business details added

  • Content: first pages published for what local customers actually search
Months 4 to 8 · Gaining Ground +

Your pages start climbing in search, the ads get sharper as we learn what makes people get in touch, and new reviews keep building trust.

  • SEO: earlier pages start ranking, newer pages build on them
  • Ads: what does not work is cut, budget moves to what brings enquiries

Months 9 to 12 · The Local Name +

You become a name people in your area find, trust and recommend, with enquiries coming in steadily while you get on with the work.

  • SEO: holding your position against competitors, while the gap over a site left alone keeps growing
  • Ads: mostly fine tuning and keeping what works running
  • Content: a steady flow of fresh proof keeps trust and rankings up

Illustrative picture of how it builds over time, actual velocity depends on your market and starting position, mapped out on the strategy call.

Pricing FAQ

Direct answers.

01Flexibility
Do I have to sign up for the monthly retainer?
No. The website build stands entirely on its own as a high-converting asset you own outright. The retainer is an optional lever for businesses that want ongoing marketing execution to compound their returns after launch.
02Flexibility
Can I change which services I'm running month to month?
Yes. There's no long lock-in contract forcing you into a fixed combination, if your priorities shift, the services and the price adjust with them, discussed directly, not buried in fine print.
03Return
How do I know this investment will pay for itself?
I don't use fabricated ROI calculators or fake guarantees. On the strategy call, I look at your current customer value, local search volume, and margins to determine realistically what it will take for your site and marketing to cover their own costs and generate a clear return.
04Ownership
What happens after the site launches if I don't take the retainer?
You own it outright. If you want to keep growing from there, that's what the retainer covers, otherwise the site is yours to run as-is, no strings attached.
