Search & SEORecommended

Getting found by parents who do not know your name yet.

$1,000 / month

Picture this. A parent who has never heard of you searches for youth soccer training near them, or asks ChatGPT who to trust, and Lazazzi Sport is the name that comes up. A family you would never have reached finds you while you are out on the field.

When a parent wants soccer training for their child, they do one of three things. They search Google, they look at the map, or more and more often they ask an AI assistant like ChatGPT or Google's AI answer box "who is a good youth soccer trainer near me". SEO is the work that puts Lazazzi Sport in all three places, and keeps you there.

What it gets you

  • Google search: showing up when parents search for youth soccer training, private soccer lessons and soccer camps in your area
  • Google Maps: your Google Business Profile set up properly and kept current, which decides who appears in the map results
  • AI search: the structured information and well written pages that ChatGPT, Google AI answers and similar tools read when they recommend a trainer. This is fast becoming one of the main ways parents choose
  • Two new pages every month, each targeting something parents actually search for, such as a camp, an age group or an area you train in
  • Technical fixes, page speed and indexing kept right, so nothing quietly stops Google listing you

If you skip it

  • The site still works and still converts anyone who already has your name
  • But you will mostly be found by people who were already going to find you
  • Whoever does put the work in will take the "near me" searches in your area
  • Honest caveat: this builds over time. Expect three to six months before it really shows, which is exactly why it is worth starting soon after launch.
Why it comes first: it is the one that keeps bringing in new families while you are on the field. It also includes the site's hosting, security and technical upkeep.
