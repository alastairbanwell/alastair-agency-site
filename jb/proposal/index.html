Search & SEOMost popular

Getting found by parents who do not know your name yet.

£650 / month
£750

When a parent in Edinburgh wants a coach, they do one of three things. They search Google, they look at the map, or more and more often they ask an AI assistant like ChatGPT or Google's AI answer box "who is a good football coach near me". SEO is the work that puts you in all three places, and keeps you there.

What it gets you

  • Google search: showing up for searches like "football coaching Edinburgh", "1 to 1 football coach near me" and "kids football camps Edinburgh"
  • Google Maps: your Google Business Profile set up properly and kept current, which is what decides who appears in the map results
  • AI search: the structured information and well written pages that ChatGPT, Google AI answers and similar tools read when they recommend a coach. This is becoming one of the main ways people choose
  • Two new pages every month, each targeting something parents actually search for, such as a session type or an area you coach in
  • Technical fixes, page speed and indexing kept right, so nothing quietly stops Google listing you

If you skip it

  • The site still works and still converts anyone who already has your name
  • But you will mostly be found by people who were already going to find you
  • Whoever does put the work in will take the "near me" searches in your area
  • Honest caveat: this builds over time. Expect three to six months before it really shows. If you want bookings next week, ads are the faster lever.
Why it is the most popular: it is the one that keeps working while you coach. It also includes the site's hosting, security and technical upkeep, so for most coaches it is the only monthly plan they need.
